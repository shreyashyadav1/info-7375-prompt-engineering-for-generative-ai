# PROMPTS: Quote It or Refuse. (four-part cut)

No generation prompts. No AI image, video or music generation was used. Every visual is drawn in
Manim from `scenes.py` (the show-tell kit, the toolkit's approved original props, and the toolkit's
logo files) or rendered from the toolkit's Remotion components (`BrutalistHesitantWriter`,
`ShowTellCard`). Narration is the local Kokoro `am_onyx` voice reading the script in `beat_sheet.json`.
There are no open pantry slots.
